# KDP Niche Pro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

KDP Niche Pro is a Chrome extension that helps self-publishers research book niches and keywords on Amazon.

## Data we collect
None. KDP Niche Pro does not collect, sell or share any personal or usage data. We run no servers and no analytics.

## Data stored on your device
Your settings, saved niches, research history and a temporary cache of Amazon search and book data are stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from the extension's Settings ("Clear cache") or by removing the extension.

## Websites the extension accesses
- **Amazon** (amazon.com and other Amazon marketplaces, and Amazon's search suggestion service): to read the public search results, book pages and bestseller lists you are researching. These requests go directly from your browser to Amazon.

## Third parties
No data is sold or shared with anyone. The only third party contacted is Amazon, for the research you request.

## Contact
Questions or requests: [open an issue on this page's GitHub repository](https://github.com/anoune08/kdp-niche-pro-privacy/issues).

KDP Niche Pro is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon.
